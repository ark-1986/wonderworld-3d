# Free visual and software resources

All 11 models shipped with WonderWorld v0.3 were created as original art using the included source generator (`tools/generate_models.py`). They do **not** rely on third-party downloads, subscriptions or paid services.

These additional packs are possible future choices, but **are not included or integrated** in this release. Check upstream license/version at download time:

- Kenney Castle Kit (CC0), official: https://kenney.nl/assets/castle-kit
- Kenney Nature Kit (CC0), official: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- Kenney City Kit (Commercial) (CC0): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial
- KayKit Adventurers (free-tier character pack, CC0, free portion distinct from extras): https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers
- Quaternius Ultimate Stylized Nature Pack (CC0): https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatestylizednature.html

Engine/hosting/build:
- Godot Engine: https://godotengine.org/ (MIT licensed)
- Cloudflare Workers & Durable Objects: https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/platform/pricing/ (Free plan with quotas; no guarantee of unlimited usage)
- GitHub Actions: https://docs.github.com/en/actions (Free quota depends on public/private repo and plan)

No paid services are necessary to develop or run limited private testing. As traffic grows, free quotas and child-safety moderation create additional operational requirements; this is not an unrestricted free Roblox replacement.
